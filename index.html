Policies

Good to know before you go

Reservation & rebooking

A ₱500 down payment is required; without it, there is no reservation. Down payments are non-refundable and non-transferable. Confirmed reservations may be rescheduled with at least two days' notice, subject to availability. Rebooking requests require at least 48 hours' notice, are valid within 30 days, and each reservation receives one free rebooking. No-shows forfeit their reservation.

House rules & quiet hours

Only registered guests are allowed. No smoking, cooking, or parties inside rooms. Keep noise reasonable; quiet hours are 10 PM–5 AM. Maximum speaker size is 2 ft high by 1 ft wide (60 × 30 cm). Guests are responsible for damage; management is not liable for lost items.

Pets, camping & safety

Maximum two pets per group in designated pet-friendly areas. Keep pets leashed or in a carrier; they are not allowed in the pool, kitchen or restricted areas. Campfires are only allowed in designated areas, must never be left unattended, and must be fully extinguished. A life vest is mandatory for falls swimming; a waiver is available for strong swimmers who decline one.

Parking

Daytour / overnight: Bike ₱10 / ₱20; Motorcycle ₱20 / ₱40; Tricycle ₱30 / ₱60; Car/4 wheels ₱50 / ₱100. Use designated spaces, do not block access ways, and secure belongings. Overnight parking is for registered guests or requires prior management approval.
